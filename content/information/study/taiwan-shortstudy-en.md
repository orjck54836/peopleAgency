Want to improve your Chinese quickly while immersing yourself in Taiwanese culture? In partnership with a professional language center in Taichung, we offer a short-term study abroad program that combines small-group Chinese instruction with authentic Taiwanese cultural experiences.

## Small-Group Chinese Instruction

![Students practicing conversational Chinese in a bright small-group classroom](/images/information/taiwan-shortstudy/class.jpg)

Working with a Taichung language center, we provide high-quality teaching resources with small class sizes (around 4–8 students), ensuring every learner receives ample guidance. Courses cover conversation, listening, and practical Chinese for study, travel, and daily life, designed by experienced instructors according to each student's level.

Small classes boost learning efficiency and encourage interaction between students, helping you build practical Chinese skills fast.

## Taiwanese Cultural Experiences

Language learning goes beyond textbooks. Our program features a variety of hands-on cultural activities:

![A student writing Spring Festival couplets, experiencing Taiwanese calligraphy](/images/information/taiwan-shortstudy/tea.jpg)

Calligraphy and Spring Festival couplet writing to feel the festive spirit of Lunar New Year; making suncakes, a Taichung specialty, alongside your instructor; night market visits where you order and chat in Chinese while tasting bubble tea and soup dumplings; and cultural walks through Taichung's temples and old streets.

These activities let students use Chinese naturally outside the classroom, reinforcing what they learn.

## Who This Program Is For

![A student arriving at the airport in Taiwan to begin their study trip](/images/information/taiwan-shortstudy/airport.jpg)

This program suits students who want intensive short-term Chinese study, international learners eager to experience Taiwanese culture, and anyone wanting to get familiar with the language and environment before long-term study. If you're planning to head to Japan afterward, explore our [complete guide to studying in Taiwan](/study/taiwan).

## Enrollment & Process

Choose from flexible 1-week, 2-week, or 4-week plans. Submit an application, pay after receiving your acceptance, and you're enrolled. Airport pickup is optional, followed by Chinese classes and cultural activities at the Taichung language center.
